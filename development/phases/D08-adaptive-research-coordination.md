# D08 — Adaptive research coordination

**State:** NOT_STARTED. This plan is a proposed scope, not execution evidence.

**Goal:** Add multiple approaches only where they improve useful progress, while preserving isolation, budgets, and coherent state.

**Dependencies:** D07

**Specification:** [project_description.md](../../project_description.md), with exact references to be reconciled in [REQUIREMENTS.md](../REQUIREMENTS.md) during D00.

## Outcomes

1. Implement coordinator planning proposals, readiness/dependency scheduling, limited branch creation, and exact recorded communication through the controller.
2. Support genuinely different approaches, conditional sharing, branch reconciliation, and targeted disagreement resolution.
3. Preserve failures and stop/reformulation reasons; bound repeated repair and exploration loops.

## Acceptance obligations

- [ ] A staged multi-approach fixture improves a specific outcome over its single-researcher path without evading budget limits.
- [ ] Conflicting assumptions prevent inappropriate branch merging; shared information is reflected in independence labels.
- [ ] The coordinator cannot promote its own unsupported result by changing status.

## Important failure cases

- Agents multiply without a bounded purpose.
- Consensus replaces a discriminating calculation.
- Late work overwrites a newer canonical context or duplicates an expensive job.

## Execution and evidence

Before execution, divide these outcomes into the smallest coherent requests with independently testable results. Do not invent every function signature months ahead of its dependencies. The active request fixes its public interfaces, exact changed paths, validation, scope, and stop conditions before CWI starts. A phase may require multiple runs. Record each final receipt/source pair, integration commit, requirement evidence, limitations, and newly deferred items.
