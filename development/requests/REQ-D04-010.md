AC-TASK-01 (R-004/R-008/R-009/R-032): Implement a complete passive immutable

identity and explicit revision, scientific objective, exact immutable inputs,

specification. Exact references retain relationship, kind, ID and positive revision;
no latest-head selector or guessed inputs. Distinguish existing inputs from expected
future outputs: do not manufacture evidence/artifact references for unproduced work.

AC-TASK-02 (R-004/R-009/R-016/R-032/R-045): Separate authored specifications and
requested roles from actual worker/attempt/session identities, current authority,
queue state, operational completion and scientific support. Explicit missing facts

immutable revision or explicitly related task; conversion cannot rewrite an issued
assignment. Define precisely which consistency facts are local and which need future
history, resource, authorization, visibility and admission checks. Keep scientific
sufficiency and completion judgments authored, rather than inventing an automatic
science oracle. No imported prose, policy reference or reported assessment becomes
a trusted grant merely by appearing in the contract.

AC-TASK-03 (R-004/R-008/R-092): Provide complete closed canonical wire formats,
