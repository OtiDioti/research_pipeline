## Settled engineering contract

1. Add immutable detached typed values for Review, Issue and their small assessment/support-route value objects. Use an explicit version-1 assessment-content discriminator distinct from the existing scientific-content discriminator; do not change the four existing codecs or reinterpret generic JSON as a review. Concrete module/signatures belong to the Architect. Preserve strict JSON/Unicode/no-coercion/exact-field behavior, explicit required collections, unknown-version rejection and useful errors. Collections retain ordering, reference roles and detached semantics. No scientific text evaluation, confidence score, formal proof language, provider dependency or new SQLite schema/backup version.
2. Review identifies one exact candidate claim, one exact target research context, a nonblank reviewer attribution, a requested/reported independence mode, shared dimensions/limitations, exact context-manifest reference, exact authorization-policy reference, reported executed-check references, a substantive assessment, open obligations, and limitations. Context manifest is a generic work_product_manifest and policy is a generic decision record in this slice: these exact references preserve declarations but do not certify exposure isolation or authority. The three supported verification modes are answer_blind_reconstruction, proof_blind_reconstruction and candidate_aware_audit from §6.5; mode is explicitly reported, never guaranteed by a string. Retain declared shared formulation/sources/lemmas/method/implementation/communications as named descriptive data, including explicit empties, without claiming independence from absent declarations.
3. A review's substantive assessment has independent work_stage, scientific_disposition, support_types, applicability and novelty dimensions. Use explicit stable codes for the §5.4 examples: work stage exploring/frozen_candidate/under_review/archived; disposition provisional/supported_within_scope/disputed/support_withdrawn/refuted_within_scope/superseded; support types heuristic/analytical/symbolic/numerical/formal/source_based (ordered unique collection); applicability applicable/requires_reassessment/inapplicable/unresolved; novelty unassessed/known_equivalent_identified/search_found_no_equivalent/researcher_assessed_contribution. All are attributed report values, not current canonical state or permissions. No ordering/confidence ladder, implicit conversion between dimensions, acceptance threshold or default promotion. Nonblank rationale and explicit conditions/obligations/limitations preserve conditional scope. Allow disagreements without selecting a winner.
4. Review carries an ordered collection of explicit support-route assessments. Each pins an exact typed Justification, a declared outcome sufficient/invalid/stale/inapplicable/incomplete/unresolved, nonblank rationale and explicit outstanding conditions/limitations. The collection may be empty (no routes assessed); no inference of sufficiency from edge count, a second route or task completion. A declared sufficient route is an attributed claim for future authorized acceptance, not an approved route. Preserve conjunctions inside the original justification; alternatives remain separate route entries. Duplicate exact route targets within a review are rejected; different reviewers/revisions may disagree about the same exact route. Invalid support is distinct from refuted claim. Conditional assessments can state insufficient premises/existence obligations without silently erasing them.
5. Issue carries nonblank disputed_point, nonempty exact affected-record references, nonblank objection_or_counterexample, a declared severity (informational/nonblocking/blocking), nonblank proposed_discriminating_action, disposition open/under_investigation/resolved/dismissed, and explicit rationale/limitations. These are attributed reports, not automatic support withdrawal, refutation or issue resolution authority. A new revision records an updated assertion, leaving the old exact target and content intact. Include exact supporting evidence references and artifact references where supplied. Both Review and Issue carry declared origin restricted to synthetic or unverified, never an authentic/human-approved flag. CreationProvenance remains caller attribution, not authentication.
6. All record/artifact semantic references project into the existing envelope links in deterministic role/order, with exact equality checked on decode and submission. Review/Issue envelopes use their existing schema-2 kinds with null canonical operational/scientific state. A review with disposition supported_within_scope still leaves its candidate's canonical claim disposition provisional and creates no eligibility/decision/permission records. Artifact evidence cannot be supplied as arbitrary paths. Review reported checks may point to exact typed Evidence or generic ToolExecution records; distinguish these declared roles and check target kinds. Such references do not certify authentic execution. Issue supporting evidence points to typed Evidence. Other affected-record references are exact generic targets and do not trigger an unbounded semantic traversal of every operational record.
7. Extend the existing RecordStore.submit_typed/get_typed_revision boundary coherently to admit/reconstruct these two new typed kinds, including mixed scientific/review/issue batches with forward references. Keep one underlying commit algorithm and preserve all previous public scientific APIs/wire bytes. Use a small explicit dispatcher/reference-role description if needed, with no mutable plugin registration or unrestricted callbacks. Resolve constrained scientific/review/issue targets under the existing transaction; generic manifest/policy/tool-execution/affected targets require existence and declared-kind checks only and confer no higher trust. The Architect must specify exactly which edges are traversed and avoid interpreting generic target payloads as approved contracts. Reject malformed reachable constrained content, missing/substituted links and unsupported versions; terminate safely on allowed descriptive cycles.
8. A review route must actually name a typed Justification whose exact conclusions include that review's candidate claim. Check this structural membership at new admission against the submitted batch or committed exact history. Do not require target context to equal the claim/route context: an inapplicability assessment can deliberately compare different exact contexts, and old-context validity must remain expressible. Preserve all three pinned contexts rather than interpreting textual equivalence. A direct review can exist with no route. Issues may refer to any existing exact generic kind; they do not imply sufficient support or invalidity. Do not impose accepted-deduction cycle policy on these provisional reports.
9. Preserve expected-head CAS and append-only histories. New assertions by independent reviewers can use separate identities; later revisions under one identity do not erase the earlier disagreement. Identical canonical submission retry returns the original receipt even after later revisions or artifact damage, before checking current targets/bytes, using the existing reconciliation contract. Conflicting ID, stale head, absent/malformed/wrong-kind target, route not concluding the candidate and missing/corrupt direct artifact fail without metadata/head/event/receipt effects. No latest-reference rebinding, replacement receipts or historical JSON rewrite. Backup/migration/generic reads remain unchanged and preserve unsupported typed-looking data without granting admission.
10. Imports inert, stdlib only, explicit outside-source runtime paths. Include a small executed synthetic usage example that stores two disagreeing reviews, a separate issue revision and unchanged candidate history, then reopens/restores them. Document reported versus verified independence/execution/authority and that atomic current eligibility/freshness and Apply disposition remain future work. This is executable record infrastructure, not additional static narrative material.
