# ADR-nnn — Decision

**State:** PROPOSED / ACCEPTED / SUPERSEDED. **Date:** actual decision date. **Authority:** who decided and within which authorization.

## Context

Problem, requirements, evidence, constraints, and what remains unknown.

## Options

Two or more genuine alternatives where they exist, with relevant consequences. Avoid manufacturing weak alternatives solely for format.

## Decision and rationale

Exact choice and why it fits current evidence. Distinguish user requirements from proposed defaults.

## Consequences and verification

Affected requirements, phases, interfaces, tests, operational risks, cost, and rollback/migration. Link accepted evidence when implemented.

## Revisit condition

What observation or requirement change would justify revisiting this choice? Link a superseding decision without erasing history.
