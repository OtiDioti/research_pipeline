## Required outcome and acceptance

AC-CONCURRENT-01 — Automatic exhaustive discovery and fixture-safe units. Preserve and extend full unittest discovery; ordinary newly added valid test modules/classes/methods automatically join the same scheduler with no registration/worker list or fixed test assignments. Preserve discovery order, inherited tests, module/class setup/teardown/cleanup, unittest expected-result and subtest semantics, errors from load_tests/imports and custom suite behavior. Never split a module/class fixture across concurrent units or rerun discovery hooks per worker. Use process-based independent execution units; fixture owner and actual dependencies govern grouping, not filenames alone. For custom/unsafe/dependent suites use a correct sequential fallback without omission. Explicit exceptional serialization metadata is permitted only if minimal, documented, dynamically discovered and conservative: no allow-list of ordinary tests. A serial group must exclude concurrent peers when its resource can conflict with them. Unsupported/ambiguous grouping fails closed or runs the intact suite sequentially, preserving its semantics. Do not promise automatic detection of arbitrary undeclared external dependencies; document the ordinary isolated-test convention and exceptional shared-resource contract honestly.

AC-CONCURRENT-02 — Resource allocation. Let A be logical CPU cores effectively available to this process, respecting actual affinity, supported process CPU-count APIs, cpusets and container quota constraints including relevant ancestry where exposed. Let T be mutually independent currently eligible units. Compute exactly C=min(T//2,max(0,A-2)); if C>0 run at most 2*C units concurrently, else run remaining units sequentially. No hardcoded worker count, zero workers, divide-by-zero or hangs on A=1/2 or T=0/1; empty suites still fail validation. Recompute against available environment/remaining eligible work at documented scheduling boundaries; running work and shrinking workload must not permit new oversubscription. Child-only CPU affinity uses at most C allowed logical CPUs, leaving two of the available set out of concurrent allocation when possible; never change coordinator/validator affinity or machine settings. Descendants inherit the child restriction. Where affinity cannot be guaranteed, enforce concurrency budget and report the limitation; unknown quota/platform constraints are stated honestly and handled conservatively. Avoid unnecessary processes for sequential/small work and excessive memory/startup overhead; include the chosen C/A/T, concurrency and affinity behavior in deterministic diagnostics.
