# Detailed development decisions

The proposed defaults are indexed in [DECISIONS.md](../../DECISIONS.md). Confirm or revise them during D00. Add an ADR using [the template](../templates/DECISION.md) for a consequential choice, material change, or resolved conflict.

Do not claim that a proposal was approved merely because it appears in the starter kit. Keep product scientific decisions inside eventual research projects, separate from these software-development decisions.

[M0 contract and scientific review packet](M0-contract-and-scientific-review.md) proposes development contracts and records the missing initial pilot/evaluation decisions. It links the delivered synthetic example and actual evidence; it is not an expert judgment or a live research-project authorization.
