# Coordinator continuation prompt

Provides a concise restart prompt for this commissioned repository; it grants no authority and does not automatically resume a stopped session.

Use after an explicit human continuation instruction. Initial commissioning and activation prompts remain in [historical startup guidance](history/development-CODEX_START.md); do not repeat commissioning in this repository.

```text
Resume top-level coordination only within my current instruction and effective human authority. Follow AGENTS.md. Begin with the current STATUS.md, development/AUTHORIZATION.md and its applicable source amendments/revocations, local/live.md if present, and the relevant run/request record.

Inspect fresh Git/CWI/actual-host ownership and preserve any live or base-bound checkout. Verify the authorized specification digest and cumulative limits. Read only the requirement rows and original specification sections needed for the next action; expand to the full specification if the hash changed, the crosswalk is incomplete or evidence conflicts.

Use DEVELOPMENT.md's ownership map and FOLDER_CONTENT.md indexes. Read historical records on demand; do not load the entire changelog, decision history or previous checkpoints by default. Archived instructions are evidence of their recorded context, not current permission.

All product changes go through CWI. For a permitted new run, verify access and use the eight role flags in MODEL_POLICY.md/RUNBOOK.md; existing runs keep frozen settings. Follow actual acceptance, integration and target-validation gates. Current records are updated in place; each execution narrative belongs once in its run record.

If current authority permits development, follow AUTONOMY.md. Otherwise stop at the human's boundary, preserve evidence and leave one next safe action. Do not infer scientific approval, reset limits or claim automatic restart.
```

[Record layout](FOLDER_CONTENT.md) and [ownership map](../DEVELOPMENT.md#4-information-ownership-avoid-competing-status-files) provide further navigation.
