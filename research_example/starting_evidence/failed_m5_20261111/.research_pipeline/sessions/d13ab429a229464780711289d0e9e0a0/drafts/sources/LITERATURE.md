# Literature and evidence assessment

Access date for all retrievals: 2026-11-11. Public retrieval used direct arXiv HTML requests; no private manuscript or notes were uploaded. No reference-project science was reused.

- E. Valvo et al., *Light-Hole Spin Qubits in Strained SiGe Lattice-Matched to Ge*, supplied manuscript dated 29 October 2026: [original PDF](../starting_evidence/Light_Hole_Spin_Qubits_in_Strained_SiGe_Lattice_Matched_to_Ge.pdf), [extracted text](../work/manuscript.txt). Full main-text PDF examined, seven pages including references; supplement absent, reference [35] is a publisher placeholder. Fig. 2 uses F=1 V/micrometre, asymmetric xGe=0.8/0.7 barriers. Eq. (2) defines alpha in energy-length units. Relevant to the actual requested device, but does not give the needed material profiles and decomposition.
- Same authors, earlier public version [arXiv:2608.25852v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25852v1): [preserved HTML](2608.25852v1.html), [readable extraction](2608.25852v1-readable.txt). Full HTML including supplement examined in relevant model/subband sections. Supplement Eq. (14) gives alpha=-6 sqrt(2) alpha0 gamma3 Im<fl|kz|fs>, explicitly an infinite-well approximation. Public version differs from the supplied October manuscript (figure panels and content); its supplement cannot be silently treated as the October supplement. Appendix A uses the same six-band modeling source below.
- P. Del Vecchio, K. Rossi, G. Scappucci and S. Bosco, *Tailoring Germanium Heterostructures for Quantum Devices with Machine Learning*, [arXiv:2604.21732](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.21732): [preserved HTML](2604.21732.html), [readable extraction](2604.21732-readable.txt). Full HTML retrieved; relevant main-text Eq. (3) and supplemental ordered Hamiltonian, basis and vertical diagonalization sections examined. Position-dependent ordering is explicit, with gamma3' and kappa' in couplings. Supplemental text says SiGe gamma and kappa use a composition interpolation from its Ref. [4], not simple linear interpolation; exact inputs to October device remain unconfirmed. The unversioned URL is recorded honestly; do not assert it equals the notes' cited v2.
- P. Del Vecchio and O. Moutanabbir, *Light-hole spin confined in germanium*, Phys. Rev. B 110, 045409 (2024), [arXiv:2402.10449](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.10449), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.110.045409), [preserved HTML](2402.10449.html). Full HTML retrieved, relevant Rashba definitions, symmetry discussion and material table examined. Table gives Ge gamma=(13.38,4.24,5.69), Delta=0.290 eV, b=-2.86 eV, c11=124 GPa, c12=41.3 GPa, aGe=5.657956 Angstrom. These are source-supported Ge values, not validated SiGe-alloy parameters.
- [Supplied human TeX notes](../starting_evidence/my_notes_on_light_holes.tex): relevant six-band matrix, interface matrices, vertical confinement, field conversion and source references examined. Evidence only, scientific status UNKNOWN; no original bytes edited. Their explicit kappa'/q' matrices provide the candidate projected terms, which must be checked against the ordered primary model. They themselves warn that finite barrier offsets cannot be inferred from compositions alone. No historical note diff exists, so alleged previous edits/deletions cannot be reconstructed.

Luttinger–Kohn (1955), Bir–Pikus (1974), Winkler (2003), Foreman (1993), Rodina et al. (2002) are cited by the notes. Their full texts were not retrieved or examined in this investigation; they are not used as claims of direct access.
